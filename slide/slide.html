05 · Spirit of the Mangroves

Four acts, one island

Players cross an illustrated map of Pulau Carey. Each act teaches one tradition by doing it.

  1. Act IMangrove Swamp
    Gather Find pandanus leaves and Nyireh Batu wood
  2. Act IIWeaver’s Hut
    Weave Follow the kelarai pattern to weave a tempok
  3. Act IIICarver’s Bench
    Carve Match carvings, then free the Chained Tiger
  4. Act IVCeremonial Fire
    Dance Keep the Main Jo’oh rhythm with bamboo stampers

06 · Inside the journey

Every actleads somewhere

  • GuideMoyang Tok NaningAn ancestral spirit tells the story and sets each quest
  • ArchiveThe Spirit ArchiveEight treasures of real history and ancestral myth, unlocked as you play
  • SupportThe artisansBuy crafts, book a Pulau Carey tour or donate to preservation
